# Decide the navigation and what a released player sees

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 02, 03

## Question

How is the dashboard navigation grouped, and which surfaces does a released player see?

## Context

- Today there are six equal tabs: Game, Settings, Prompt Presets, Worlds, Games and LLM Messages. The owner plays and tunes equally now. A released player plays first and tunes less (`PRODUCT.md`).
- `PRODUCT.md` lists this as an open decision: "how much of the tuning surface a released player sees". Resolving this ticket updates `PRODUCT.md`.
- A possible grouping: a Play area (Game, Games) and an Author area (Worlds, Prompt Presets, Settings, LLM Messages). "Play area" and "Author area" are working names (map Notes, "Names").
- Inputs: the scores of [Score the five other tabs](02-score-the-other-tabs.md), and the direction from [Choose the direction of the Game tab](03-choose-the-direction.md).
- The critique found that the Game tab needs the Games tab for Narrator Mode and the presets, and that the tab bar overflows at phone width.
- Use `/grilling` and `/domain-modeling`: a name that reaches product copy is a `CONTEXT.md` decision.

Done when the answer records the grouping, what a released player sees, and the update to `PRODUCT.md`, and the fog "The layouts of the five other tabs" can graduate into tickets.
