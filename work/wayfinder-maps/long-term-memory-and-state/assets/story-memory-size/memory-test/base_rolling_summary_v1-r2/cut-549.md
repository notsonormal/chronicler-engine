# Memory — Story So Far

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate (Islaport) after grandfather Bernard's death. Meets bodyguard Carla at the gate; she asks to borrow his phone for threat assessment. He keeps it in his hand — she looks, he unlocks. Login history shows an entry three days ago, after his train booking, from near here; he told only his mother he was coming. Carla: don't clear history, copy the timestamp.

**Master key:** one passcode Bernard held, opens all estate locks (accounts, foundation books, vault, doors). Handoff procedure: vault room off the old dispensary; lawyer Campion witnesses, Jezebel runs it, new code set from a sealed slip only Bernard read, slip destroyed. In and out in an hour. Carla says Jezebel is an ally, not a suspect; keep the login from her a day to see if it leaks.

They go to the gatehouse (coffee, gun oil, monitor bank: drive, rear service road, pier gate), then the east wing. In the marble hall, **Gabriella** intercepts — lavender gown, pearls — offers a house tour; mentions a draft in the east wing. Julian declines, says he has business with Jezebel. Carla notes there is no draft — a warning or lure; they don't investigate today.

**Jezebel's office** (east wing, ink and old paper, ledgers, window on formal gardens). Julian asks to see the handoff procedure end to end, playing the worried heir. Jezebel reveals: four years ago a retired locksmith serviced the vault door with it propped open and had the slip in hand; she signed off on the visit. Julian tells her about the three-day-old login. She names suspects/leads: Bernard's brother Edmund (dead twelve years), solicitor Campion (paperwork only), and Marguerite, Bernard's nurse for his last two years, who was told things Jezebel wasn't. Bernard kept a log in the bottom drawer of his private desk in the study. Jezebel never opened it; says Julian may have cause now.

**Plans:** Julian to walk the house on his own time after Jezebel; Gabriella expects him in the west parlor this afternoon. Carla's cue: flag any procedure step that doesn't match her memory.
