# Memory — Story So Far

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate (Islaport) after grandfather Bernard's death. Bodyguard Carla at the gate; login history shows an entry three days ago from near here; only his mother knew he was coming. Carla: don't clear history, copy the timestamp.

**Master key:** one passcode Bernard held, opens all estate locks. Handoff: vault room off the old dispensary; lawyer Campion witnesses, Jezebel runs it, new code from a sealed slip only Bernard read, slip destroyed. In and out in an hour. Carla says Jezebel is an ally; keep the login from her a day to see if it leaks.

Gatehouse (coffee, gun oil, monitor bank), then east wing. **Gabriella** intercepts in the marble hall — lavender gown, pearls — offers a tour, mentions a draft in the east wing. Julian declines; Carla notes there is no draft — a warning or lure; not investigated today.

**Jezebel's office.** Julian asks to see the handoff procedure end to end. Jezebel reveals: four years ago a retired locksmith serviced the vault door with it propped open and had the slip in hand; she signed off. Julian tells her about the three-day-old login. She names leads: Bernard's brother Edmund (dead twelve years), solicitor Campion, and Marguerite, Bernard's nurse for his last two years. Bernard kept a log in the bottom drawer of his private desk in the study; Jezebel never opened it.

**Study (Julian, Jezebel, Carla at the door):** Jezebel unlocks the bottom drawer — empty, no scrap of paper. Julian checks for a false bottom; nothing. Jezebel: Bernard kept things in two places, the desk and the reading chair in the library. Under the chair's green cushion: a small brass key with a tag stamped *Hollis* in Bernard's hand.

Jezebel: Hollis is Marguerite Hollis, Edmund's widow, on Marchand Street in town; Bernard sent her money every quarter for twelve years, quiet, and she never wrote back. Ledger shows modest quarterly payments, last one noted "For the boy's schooling" — she had a son, now near eighteen. Payments went through Marchand Mutual bank. Jezebel will ring the bank before noon to see if the last draught cleared or sits uncollected.

**Plan:** Julian, Carla (and possibly Jezebel) take the carriage to Marchand Street today, before word the drawer was opened travels. Line agreed: Bernard left Julian the key with her name on it; nothing about the drawer or the login. If she asks why now, tell the truth as far as it goes. Julian wants to hear the son out if he's there.

**Carla's advice:** widow's door — knock, who you are, why you've come, in one breath, then leave a gap. Son — cards down plain and early: the key and the money. Carla hangs back a step at the gate.

**Arrival:** green gate (chalky paint, sagging hinge), neat garden, blue door, polished knocker. A tall thin young man, dirt to the wrist, pruning knife in hand, Redmist brow, steps out: "Can I help you?" Carla waits at the gate.
