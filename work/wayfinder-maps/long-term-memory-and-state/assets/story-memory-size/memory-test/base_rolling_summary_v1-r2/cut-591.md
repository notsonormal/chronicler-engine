# Memory — Story So Far

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate (Islaport) after grandfather Bernard's death. Bodyguard Carla at the gate; login history shows an entry three days ago from near here; only his mother knew he was coming. Carla: don't clear history, copy the timestamp.

**Master key:** one passcode Bernard held, opens all estate locks. Handoff: vault room off the old dispensary; lawyer Campion witnesses, Jezebel runs it, new code from a sealed slip only Bernard read, slip destroyed. In and out in an hour. Carla says Jezebel is an ally; keep the login from her a day to see if it leaks.

**Gabriella** intercepts in the marble hall — lavender gown, pearls — offers a tour, mentions a draft in the east wing. Carla notes there is no draft — a warning or lure; not investigated today.

**Jezebel's office.** Julian asks to see the handoff procedure end to end. Jezebel reveals: four years ago a retired locksmith serviced the vault door with it propped open and had the slip in hand; she signed off. Julian tells her about the three-day-old login. She names leads: Bernard's brother Edmund (dead twelve years), solicitor Campion, and Marguerite, Bernard's nurse for his last two years. Bernard kept a log in the bottom drawer of his private desk in the study; Jezebel never opened it.

**Study (Julian, Jezebel, Carla at the door):** bottom drawer empty. Under the green cushion of the library reading chair: a small brass key with a tag stamped *Hollis* in Bernard's hand. Jezebel: Marguerite Hollis is Edmund's widow, on Marchand Street in town; Bernard sent her money every quarter for twelve years, quiet, never a reply. Ledger: modest quarterly payments, last noted "For the boy's schooling" — she had a son, now near eighteen. Payments went through Marchand Mutual bank. Jezebel will ring the bank before noon to see if the last draught cleared or sits uncollected.

**Plan:** Julian, Carla (and possibly Jezebel) take the carriage to Marchand Street today, before word the drawer was opened travels. Line agreed: Bernard left Julian the key with her name on it; nothing about the drawer or the login. Julian wants to hear the son out if he's there. Carla's advice: knock, who you are, why you've come, in one breath, then leave a gap; cards down plain and early with the son; Carla hangs back a step at the gate.

**At the gate (Julian, maid, gatekeeper):** Julian says he's bound for Marchand Street, east side, and asks them to say it plain if they'll send word ahead. Maid: half the staff is from around here, east side especially; it will travel. Gatekeeper: "ma'am" (Gabriella) has been here two weeks and talks freely before staff. Julian: tell it straight — the master went to call on a widow of the family name, more when he's done; hold the telling until the carriage is out of sight. Carla: gatekeeper has a cousin on the east side; small city.

**Marchand Street:** driver stops short of the house; Carla waits at the gate. Tomas Hollis (tall, thin, Redmist brow, trowel, dirt to the wrist) answers. Julian gives his name, the key with his mother's name on it, and why he's come; asks if she's home. Tomas: "Hollis was my father's family. She kept the name." Julian wipes his boots, says the key stays with him whether or not she'll see him. Tomas goes in; returns: "She'll see you."

**Parlor (Julian, Marguerite, Tomas in the doorway):** Marguerite Hollis — gray hair pinned, shawl, nurse's economy. Julian lays out the twelve years of quarterly payments, the "boy's schooling" note, and the key, tag up. Marguerite: the key is to **Edmund's strongbox**; the bank held it after Edmund died and wouldn't open it to her without a court, and she hadn't the money or stomach; Bernard said he'd see it sorted before he got too sick to leave the house. The box is Tomas's now. Julian: Tomas should turn the key. Asks which bank. Marguerite: Marchand Mutual held Edmund's accounts, but the strongbox went to the **Islaport Trust** when the old branch closed years back — bigger, takes instructions, a deputy manager and a rulebook, court order unless the paper names Julian. Tomas asks whether Bernard put Julian's or Tomas's name on the box. Julian: doesn't know; the will named him the fortune but no strongbox and no Tomas; the lawyer may not have known. Proposal: go to the Trust tomorrow with Tomas at his side; if paper is wanted, ask what paper, and Julian pays for a lawyer — his coin, not Marguerite's. Tomas may keep the key and go alone if he prefers.

**Agreed:** Tomas keeps the key; meet at the Islaport Trust at **1 p.m. tomorrow (Day 2)**; Julian brings the lawyer's name and whatever paper the will gave him. Tomas: mornings are no good, the clerk's window opens at nine with a queue; afternoons are quiet. On the step Tomas says Marguerite will spend tonight arguing him out of it — she's not against Julian, she's against hoping. Julian: if she talks him round, send word to the estate and no hard feelings.

**Carriage back (Julian, Carla):** Carla's read — the boy is steady, steadier than expected; Marguerite won't fuss over the key, twelve years of quiet. One o'clock at the Trust leaves the rest of today and a morning: time for Jezebel to ring the bank and learn whether the last draught was collected, and to put word among the Trust's staff that a Redmist will ask after Hollis by name. Julian's read: Tomas decided for himself, will hold under pressure, but has no experience with men like deputy managers; Julian will watch the deputy, not the box, and make sure nobody talks Tomas out of holding the key. Carla: they need a lawyer who has the Trust's rulebook half-memorized; Jezebel will know who.

**Back at the estate (gatekeeper, Julian, Carla):** gatekeeper hands Julian a folded note from Jezebel, to be given the moment he was back. Gatekeeper reports the staff know about Marchand — told straight, as Julian asked.
