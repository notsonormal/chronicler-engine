**Day 1, late morning.** In the study, Julian tells Jezebel he has cause to open Bernard's log. She insists on coming, Carla at the door; if the log is gone or rearranged they go to Campion, not police. The bottom drawer is unlocked and empty. Julian checks for a false bottom; nothing. Jezebel says Bernard kept things in the desk and in a cushion in the library reading chair. Under the cushion: a small brass key tagged *Hollis* in Bernard's hand. Jezebel: Marguerite Hollis is Edmund's widow, a former hospital nurse; Edmund died twelve years ago; Bernard sent her money quarterly, quietly, and she never replied. She lives on Marchand Street, green garden gate. Bernard's ledger shows quarterly payments for twelve years, modest, the last noted "For the boy's schooling" — she had a son, now about eighteen. Julian plans to go today, before word spreads. Line agreed: Bernard left Julian the key; no mention of the drawer or the three-days-ago login. Jezebel will ring Marchand Mutual (the paying bank) before noon to see if the last draught cleared. Carla: take the carriage, not walk; forty minutes on foot. Jezebel's advice: a widow reads faces; mean what you say. Carla's advice: to the widow — knock, name, reason in one breath, leave a gap; to the son — cards down plain and early. Julian, Carla and Jezebel part in the entrance hall; Carla fetches the carriage.

At the gate, Julian tells the maid and gatekeeper he goes to Marchand Street, east side, to call on a widow of the family name; asks the telling wait until the carriage is out of sight. Maid: half the staff is from the east side; it will travel by tonight. Gatekeeper's cousin lives on the east side (Carla notes it). They take the carriage; Carla says Jezebel keeps the bank — she'll ask as the estate's accountant; they stay out of Marchand Mutual.

At the Marchand Street house (green gate, chalky paint, sagging hinge, blue door, polished knocker), Tomas Hollis — tall, thin, trowel, dirt to the wrist, Redmist brow — opens the door. Julian gives his name, says Bernard left him a key tagged Hollis, asks what it opens. Tomas lets him in; Carla waits at the gate. Tomas: "Hollis was my father's family. She kept the name." Julian says the key stays with him whatever she decides. Tomas fetches his mother.

Marguerite Hollis, by the window, shawl, gray hair: "Bernard's grandson." Julian tells her of the twelve years of payments, the last "for the boy's schooling," and sets the key, tag up, on the table. She says it is Edmund's strongbox; the bank held it after his death and would not open it to her without a court; Bernard said he'd see it sorted, then the quarters came and no letters. "It's the boy's now." She says Marchand Mutual held Edmund's accounts; the strongbox went to the Islaport Trust when the old branch closed; a deputy manager and a rulebook want a court order unless the paper names Julian. Tomas asks whether Bernard put Julian's or Tomas's name on the box; Julian says he doesn't know — the will named him the fortune, not the box or Tomas.

Agreed: Tomas keeps the key; meet at the Islaport Trust at one o'clock tomorrow afternoon (mornings are crowded); Julian brings the lawyer's name and the will's paper, pays for a lawyer if needed. Tomas warns his mother will argue against hoping. Julian: if she talks him round, send word to the estate.

Back at the estate, the gatekeeper gives Julian a folded note from Jezebel, to be read at once; the staff were told about Marchand straight, as Julian asked. Carla: Jezebel will know a lawyer who knows the Trust's rulebook.
