**Day 1, morning.** At the Redmist Estate gate, Carla introduces herself to Julian as his bodyguard, inside and outside the estate. She asks to borrow his phone for a threat assessment — contacts, accounts, anything exposed — promising nothing is deleted or leaves her hands, and he watches. Julian agrees but holds the phone himself, unlocking only what she needs. In Settings/security they find a login from three days ago, after his train booking, not from him. Carla says someone knew his travel timeline; she has him lock the phone but keep the history, wanting a copy of the timestamp. She explains the master key: one passcode Bernard held that opens every estate lock — accounts, foundation books, vault, doors. The handoff procedure: Jezebel runs it, lawyer Campion witnesses, new code set in the vault room from a sealed slip only Bernard read, destroyed after. Carla says Jezebel is an ally, not a suspect; they keep the login from her a day to see if it leaks from her office. Plan: ask Jezebel straight about the procedure, letting her think it's new-heir nerves. Julian's rule: he listens, Carla gives the cue if a step doesn't match.

In the gatehouse (coffee, gun oil, monitor bank: drive, rear service road, pier gate) they agree on the approach. In the marble hallway, Gabriella (lavender gown, pearls) intercepts, offering a house tour and warning of a draft in the east wing. Julian declines, citing business with Jezebel. Carla notes there is no draft — a warning or lure; they won't investigate today.

In Jezebel's east-wing office (ink, paper, ledgers, window on formal gardens), Julian asks to see the inheritance and the master-key procedure end to end. Jezebel asks who told him; he says an old friend of Bernard's. She reveals a locksmith serviced the mechanism four years ago with the door propped open and the slip in hand; she signed off on the visit. Julian tells her about the three-days-ago login, made before he told anyone in Islaport. Jezebel names those Bernard might have trusted: his dead brother Edmund; Campion (paperwork only); and Marguerite, his nurse of his last two years, who was told things. She says Bernard wrote everything down — a log in the bottom drawer of his private desk in the study. She never opened it, but says Julian may have cause now.
