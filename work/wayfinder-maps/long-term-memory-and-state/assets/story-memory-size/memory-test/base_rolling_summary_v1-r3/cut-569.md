**Day 1, late morning.** In the study, Julian tells Jezebel he has cause to open Bernard's log. She insists on coming, Carla at the door; if the log is gone or rearranged they go to Campion, not police. The bottom drawer is unlocked and empty. Julian checks for a false bottom; nothing. Jezebel says Bernard kept things in the desk and in a cushion in the library reading chair. Under the cushion: a small brass key tagged *Hollis* in Bernard's hand. Jezebel: Marguerite Hollis is Edmund's widow, a former hospital nurse; Edmund died twelve years ago; Bernard sent her money quarterly, quietly, and she never replied. She lives on Marchand Street, green garden gate. Bernard's ledger shows quarterly payments for twelve years, modest, the last noted "For the boy's schooling" — she had a son, now about eighteen. Julian plans to go today, before word spreads. Line agreed: Bernard left Julian the key; no mention of the drawer or the three-days-ago login. Jezebel will ring Marchand Mutual (the paying bank) before noon to see if the last draught cleared. Carla: take the carriage, not walk; forty minutes on foot. Jezebel's advice: a widow reads faces; mean what you say. Carla's advice: to the widow — knock, name, reason in one breath, leave a gap; to the son — cards down plain and early. Julian, Carla and Jezebel part in the entrance hall; Carla fetches the carriage.

At the Marchand Street house (green gate, chalky paint, sagging hinge, blue door, polished knocker), a tall thin young man with a pruning knife and dirt to the wrist opens the door, asks "Can I help you?" Carla waits at the gate.
