# Memory — Story So Far

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate, Islaport, after grandfather Bernard's death. Meets bodyguard Carla at the gate; she is assigned to him inside and outside the estate. Julian hands over his phone but keeps it in his own hand — she looks, he unlocks. Passcode stays his.

**Phone check (Carla + Julian, gatehouse):** Login history shows an entry three days ago, after Julian booked his train — not him. Carla: someone knows his timeline; either shadowed the booking or pulled it from the rail company. She says lock it, don't clear history, she wants a copy of the timestamp. Julian notes only his mother knew he was coming.

**Master key:** One credential Bernard held that opens every estate lock — accounts, foundation books, vault, doors. Passes to Julian by procedure: vault room off the old dispensary; lawyer Campion witnesses, Jezebel runs it, new code set on the spot from a sealed slip only Bernard read, slip destroyed after. In and out in an hour. Carla: Jezebel is an ally, not a suspect; keep the login from her one day to see if it leaks from her office.

**Plan:** Ask Jezebel straight about the procedure, framing it as new-heir nerves. Julian listens; Carla signals if a step doesn't match what she remembers.

**Hallway:** Gabriella (lavender gown, pearls) intercepts, offers to tour the house, claims the east wing has a draft. Carla says there is no draft — a warning or lure. Julian declines the tour, says he'll walk the house on his own time. Gabriella says she'll be in the west parlor this afternoon. Julian plans to check the east wing later.

**Jezebel's office (Julian, Carla, Jezebel):** Julian asks to see the procedure end to end. Jezebel reveals: four years ago a retired locksmith serviced the vault door with it propped open and had the slip in hand; Jezebel signed off on the visit. Julian tells her about the three-day-old login. Jezebel names possible confidants: Bernard's brother Edmund (dead twelve years), solicitor Campion (paperwork only), and Marguerite, Bernard's nurse for his last two years, who was told things Jezebel wasn't. She says Bernard kept a log in the bottom drawer of his private desk in the study; she never opened it, but Julian may have cause.

**Known to:** Carla and Julian know of the login; Jezebel now knows too. Gabriella does not.
