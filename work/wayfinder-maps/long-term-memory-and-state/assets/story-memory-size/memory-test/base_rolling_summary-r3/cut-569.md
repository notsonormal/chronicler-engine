# Memory — Story So Far

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate, Islaport, after grandfather Bernard's death. Bodyguard Carla assigned to him inside and outside the estate. Phone check at gatehouse: a login three days ago, after Julian booked his train — not him. Carla: someone knows his timeline; lock it, don't clear history, she wants the timestamp. Only his mother knew he was coming.

**Master key:** one credential Bernard held, opens every estate lock. Passes to Julian by procedure in the vault room off the old dispensary; lawyer Campion witnesses, Jezebel runs it, new code from a sealed slip, slip destroyed. Carla: Jezebel is an ally, not a suspect; keep the login from her one day to see if it leaks from her office.

**Hallway:** Gabriella (lavender gown, pearls) offers a house tour, claims a draft in the east wing. Carla says there is no draft — warning or lure. Julian declines, says he'll walk the house himself. Gabriella says she'll be in the west parlor this afternoon. Julian plans to check the east wing later.

**Jezebel's office (Julian, Carla, Jezebel):** Jezebel reveals a retired locksmith serviced the vault door four years ago with the slip in hand; she signed off. She names confidants: Edmund (Bernard's brother, dead twelve years), Campion (paperwork only), and Marguerite, Bernard's nurse for his last two years. She tells Julian of Bernard's log in the bottom drawer of the study desk. Julian tells her of the login; Jezebel now knows.

**Study (Julian, Carla, Jezebel):** bottom drawer unlocked and empty. Jezebel: Bernard kept things in the desk and under the cushion of his library reading chair. Under the cushion: a small brass key, tag stamped *Hollis* — Marguerite Hollis, Edmund's widow, on Marchand Street, green gate. Bernard sent her money quarterly for twelve years via Marchand Mutual; last payment noted "For the boy's schooling." She has a son, near eighteen now. Jezebel will ring the bank before noon to see if the last draught cleared.

**Line agreed:** Bernard left Julian the key with her name on it. Nothing about the drawer or the login. Julian will tell her the truth as far as it goes.

**Carla's counsel:** carriage, not on foot. Widow: knock, name, reason in one breath, leave the gap. Son: cards down plain — the key and the money. Carla hangs back a step at the gate.

**Now:** Day 1, late morning. Carriage to Marchand Street; green gate, blue door. A tall thin young man, dirt to the wrist, pruning knife in hand, answers — his mother's plain face, something of the Redmist in the brow. "Can I help you?" Carla at the gate.
