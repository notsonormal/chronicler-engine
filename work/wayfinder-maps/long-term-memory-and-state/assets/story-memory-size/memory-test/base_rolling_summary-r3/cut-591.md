# Memory — Story So Far

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate, Islaport, after grandfather Bernard's death. Bodyguard Carla assigned to him. Phone check at gatehouse: a login three days ago, after Julian booked his train — not him. Only his mother knew he was coming. Carla: lock it, don't clear history, she wants the timestamp.

**Master key:** one credential Bernard held, opens every estate lock. Passed to Julian in the vault room off the old dispensary; lawyer Campion witnesses, Jezebel runs it, new code from a sealed slip, slip destroyed. Carla: Jezebel is an ally; keep the login from her one day to see if it leaks from her office.

**Hallway:** Gabriella (lavender gown, pearls) offers a house tour, claims a draft in the east wing. Carla says there is no draft — warning or lure. Julian declines, plans to check the east wing later. Gabriella will be in the west parlor this afternoon.

**Jezebel's office (Julian, Carla, Jezebel):** Jezebel reveals a retired locksmith serviced the vault door four years ago with the slip in hand; she signed off. Confidants: Edmund (Bernard's brother, dead twelve years), Campion (paperwork only), Marguerite, Bernard's nurse for his last two years. Bernard's log was in the bottom drawer of the study desk. Julian tells her of the login.

**Study (Julian, Carla, Jezebel):** bottom drawer unlocked and empty. Under the library reading chair cushion: small brass key, tag stamped *Hollis* — Marguerite Hollis, Edmund's widow, Marchand Street, green gate. Bernard sent her money quarterly for twelve years via Marchand Mutual; last payment noted "For the boy's schooling." She has a son, near eighteen. Jezebel will ring the bank before noon to see if the last draught cleared.

**Line agreed:** Bernard left Julian the key with her name on it. Nothing about the drawer or the login.

**Gate (Julian, maid, gatekeeper):** Julian tells the staff plainly he goes to Marchand Street to call on a widow of the family name; asks the telling wait until the carriage is out of sight. Gatekeeper has a cousin on the east side.

**Marchand Street, late morning (Julian, Carla at the gate, Tomas, then Marguerite):** Tomas Hollis answers — tall, thin, the Redmist brow; Edmund's son, keeps mother's name. Marguerite: the key opens Edmund's strongbox, held by Islaport Trust since the old Marchand Mutual branch closed; she couldn't open it without a court. Whatever's inside belongs to Tomas. Bernard never wrote a line about the payments. No paper names the box or Tomas.

**Appointment:** Day 2, 1 p.m., Islaport Trust — Julian and Tomas (bringing the key) meet the deputy manager. Julian pays for a lawyer if paper is needed; will bring the will and a lawyer's name. Tomas warns his mother will argue against hoping overnight; if he's talked round, he sends word to the estate.

**Now:** Day 1, midday. Carriage back at the estate. Gatekeeper hands Julian a folded note from Jezebel, to be given the moment he returned. Staff already know about Marchand, told straight.
