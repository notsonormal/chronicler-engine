# Memory — Story So Far

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate, Islaport, after grandfather Bernard's death. Bodyguard Carla assigned. Phone check at gatehouse: a login three days ago, after Julian booked his train — not him. Only his mother knew he was coming. Carla: lock it, don't clear history, she wants the timestamp.

**Master key:** one credential Bernard held, opens every estate lock. Passed to Julian in the vault room off the old dispensary; lawyer Campion witnesses, Jezebel runs it, new code from a sealed slip, slip destroyed. Carla: Jezebel is an ally; keep the login from her one day to see if it leaks from her office.

**Hallway:** Gabriella (lavender gown, pearls) offers a house tour, claims a draft in the east wing. Carla says there is no draft — warning or lure. Julian declines, plans to check the east wing later. Gabriella will be in the west parlor this afternoon.

**Jezebel's office:** Jezebel reveals a retired locksmith serviced the vault door four years ago with the slip in hand; she signed off. Confidants: Edmund (Bernard's brother, dead twelve years), Campion (paperwork only), Marguerite, Bernard's nurse for his last two years. Bernard's log was in the bottom drawer of the study desk. Julian tells her of the login.

**Study:** bottom drawer unlocked and empty. Under the library reading chair cushion: small brass key, tag stamped *Hollis* — Marguerite Hollis, Edmund's widow, Marchand Street, green gate. Bernard sent her money quarterly for twelve years via Marchand Mutual; last payment noted "For the boy's schooling." She has a son, near eighteen. Jezebel will ring the bank before noon.

**Line agreed:** Bernard left Julian the key with her name on it. Nothing about the drawer or the login.

**Gate:** Julian tells the staff plainly he goes to Marchand Street to call on a widow of the family name; asks the telling wait until the carriage is out of sight. Gatekeeper has a cousin on the east side.

**Marchand Street, late morning (Julian, Carla at the gate, Tomas, then Marguerite):** Tomas Hollis answers — tall, thin, the Redmist brow; Edmund's son, keeps mother's name. Marguerite: the key opens Edmund's strongbox, held by Islaport Trust since the old Marchand Mutual branch closed; she couldn't open it without a court. Whatever's inside belongs to Tomas. Bernard never wrote a line about the payments. No paper names the box or Tomas.

**Appointment:** Day 2, 1 p.m., Islaport Trust — Julian and Tomas (bringing the key) meet the deputy manager. Julian pays for a lawyer if paper is needed; will bring the will and a lawyer's name. Tomas warns his mother will argue against hoping overnight; if he's talked round, he sends word to the estate.

**Day 1, midday–evening.** Back at the estate, gatekeeper hands Julian Jezebel's note: last draught cleared three weeks ago, after the funeral, before the will was read; account never formally closed — keep it open, a closed account leaves a trail, an open one a door. Lawyer named: Halvorsen, old and methodical, has beaten the Trust's rulebook; Jezebel rings him tonight. Carla: watch the account for who signs the draughts now.

**Campion (telephone, Julian and Carla listening):** master-key handoff Friday, 10 a.m., before offices open; bring only himself; vault wants his key and Jezebel's. Bernard's codicil, six lines, altered in his last month: a trust "established in the winter of the year of the Draught," beneficiaries known only to Bernard and his banker; no inquiry to be treated as suspicious. Campion knew nothing of it until this afternoon; has sat on it since three o'clock. The Draught = winter 1905, harbor froze. Bernard kept accounts at Marchand Mutual, then Islaport Trust; the old account left open. Codicil stays quiet; Halvorsen hears it from Julian.

**Plans:** Carla takes the codicil to Tomas tonight, out by the kitchen side gate to the lane, hedge at his back; brings Julian the answer either way. Halvorsen sees Julian at 8 a.m. Day 2, King Street; leave by 7:30; Carla's advice — drink the tea, mind the silences, tell the whole of Marchand, don't shorten. Tomas to be at the Trust by 12:30, bench opposite the door, hears the codicil from paper first.

**House:** Julian's sitting room is the south-corridor study (Bernard's old sitting room), fire lit, telephone on the desk. Lisette: east corridor clock runs nine minutes fast; the study's west window doesn't shut true — fog makes a knocking sound, it's the wind. Louise coming up about supper; sober dark clothes for the morning.
