# Memory — Story So Far

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate, Islaport, after grandfather Bernard's death. Bodyguard Carla assigned. Phone login three days ago, after Julian booked his train — not him; only his mother knew he was coming. Carla: lock it, don't clear history.

**Master key:** one credential Bernard held, opens every estate lock. Passed to Julian in the vault room; lawyer Campion witnesses, Jezebel runs it, new code from a sealed slip, slip destroyed. Carla: Jezebel is an ally; keep the login from her one day to see if it leaks from her office.

**Hallway:** Gabriella offers a tour, claims a draft in the east wing; Carla says there is no draft. Julian declined; Gabriella in the west parlor this afternoon.

**Jezebel's office:** a retired locksmith serviced the vault door four years ago with the slip in hand; she signed off. Confidants: Edmund (Bernard's brother, dead twelve years), Campion, Marguerite (Bernard's nurse, last two years). Bernard's log was in the study desk's bottom drawer — found unlocked and empty. Under the library reading-chair cushion: small brass key tagged *Hollis* — Marguerite Hollis, Edmund's widow, Marchand Street, green gate. Bernard sent her money quarterly twelve years via Marchand Mutual; last note "For the boy's schooling." She has a son near eighteen. Jezebel rings the bank.

**Line agreed:** Bernard left Julian the key with her name on it. Nothing about the drawer or the login.

**Marchand Street, late morning (Julian, Carla at the gate, Tomas, then Marguerite):** Tomas Hollis — Edmund's son, keeps mother's name. The key opens Edmund's strongbox at Islaport Trust (old Marchand Mutual branch closed); she couldn't open it without a court. Whatever's inside belongs to Tomas. No paper names the box or Tomas.

**Appointment:** Day 2, 1 p.m., Islaport Trust — Julian and Tomas (bringing the key) meet the deputy manager. Julian pays for a lawyer if paper is needed.

**Day 1, midday–evening.** Gatekeeper hands Julian Jezebel's note: last draught cleared three weeks ago, after the funeral, before the will was read; account never formally closed — keep it open, a closed account leaves a trail. Lawyer named: Halvorsen, old and methodical, has beaten the Trust's rulebook; Jezebel rings him tonight. Carla: watch who signs the draughts now.

**Campion (telephone, Julian and Carla listening):** master-key handoff Friday, 10 a.m., before offices open; bring only himself; vault wants his key and Jezebel's. Bernard's codicil, six lines, altered in his last month: a trust "established in the winter of the year of the Draught," beneficiaries known only to Bernard and his banker; no inquiry to be treated as suspicious. Campion knew nothing until this afternoon. The Draught = winter 1905, harbor froze. Codicil stays quiet; Halvorsen hears it from Julian.

**Plans:** Carla takes the codicil to Tomas tonight by the kitchen side gate. Halvorsen sees Julian at 8 a.m. Day 2, King Street; leave by 7:30; drink the tea, mind the silences, tell the whole of Marchand. Tomas at the Trust by 12:30, bench opposite the door, hears the codicil from paper first.

**House:** Julian's sitting room is the south-corridor study, fire lit, telephone on the desk. Lisette: east corridor clock runs nine minutes fast; the study's west window doesn't shut true — fog makes a knocking sound, it's the wind. Louise (cook): pot on from six in the morning till ten at night; cold plates under cloth between; if out past ten, ring down before seven. Julian told her his hours: 8 a.m. Halvorsen, King Street; 1 p.m. Islaport Trust. Dark sober clothes laid out for the morning.

**Day 1, night.** Tomas comes to the library himself with the key, refusing to wait. He tells Julian what his father told him: the frozen harbor of the Draught, Bernard and a young hospital doctor and Edmund going through a list till dawn — coal, children, which boats to save; Edmund kept the accounts in a book, "the only honest ledger he ever kept." Tomas wrote out his father's account on paper (creased into quarters, in his coat) and offers it to the banker. Julian: the ledger itself is the paper wanted. Edmund's book is in Marguerite's chest in her room with letters and death notices; she has never let Tomas read it in twelve years. Tomas goes home tonight to ask her, as the son kept from his father's hand, not for the banker's desk. Julian: the strongbox goes to the Trust at one o'clock book or no book; if she keeps the ledger, ask the deputy manager to open the box without it.

**Carla's counsel:** a widow's bedroom is still her bedroom; want it, ask, take no until there's a court or the son's word. Julian agrees; no book out of a widow's chest.

**Day 2, half past five, morning.** Julian and Carla take the carriage to Marchand Street early, before the street wakes, to ask Marguerite to her face — not for the book, for the asking. One window lit. Julian stands short of the green gate in the lamplight, unlatched, and calls up: he came to ask to her face and hear the no to his face; nobody's arm needs twisting.
