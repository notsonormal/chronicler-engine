# Memory — Story So Far

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate, Islaport, after grandfather Bernard's death. Meets bodyguard Carla at the gate; she pledges to guard him inside and outside the estate. Julian hands over his phone but keeps it in his own hand — she looks, he unlocks. Carla checks login history: an entry three days ago, after Julian booked his train, from near Islaport. Julian had told only his mother he was coming. Carla says lock it, don't clear history; she wants a copy of the timestamp.

**Master key:** one passcode Bernard held, opening all estate locks — accounts, foundation books, vault, doors. Handoff procedure: vault room off the old dispensary; lawyer Campion witnesses, Jezebel runs it, new code set from a sealed slip only Bernard read, slip destroyed after. Carla says Jezebel is an ally, not a suspect; keep the login from her one day to see if it leaks. Plan: ask Jezebel straight about the procedure, let her think Julian's caution is nerves.

**In the marble hall:** Gabriella (lavender gown, pearls) intercepts, offers to tour the house, mentions a draft in the east wing. Julian declines — business with Jezebel first, then he'll walk the house himself. Gabriella says she'll be in the west parlor this afternoon. Carla notes there is no draft in the east wing — a warning or lure; they don't bite today, but Julian wants to learn what she'd rather he not see.

**Jezebel's office, east wing** (Julian and Carla present): Julian asks to see the inheritance and the master-key procedure end to end before setting a code. Jezebel asks who told him; Julian says an old friend of Bernard's who knew how he thought. Jezebel reveals a locksmith serviced the vault door four years ago with the door propped open and the slip in hand; she signed off on the visit. Julian tells her about the login three days ago. Jezebel names possible confidants: Bernard's brother Edmund (dead twelve years), solicitor Campion (paperwork only), and Marguerite, Bernard's nurse for his last two years, who was told things Jezebel wasn't. She says Bernard wrote everything down — a log in the bottom drawer of his private desk in the study. She never opened it; neither should Julian without cause, but he may have cause now.

**Known to whom:** Carla knows the login and the procedure; Jezebel now knows the login too. Gabriella knows nothing of either. Julian and Carla plan to review the study log and investigate the east-wing "draft" later.
