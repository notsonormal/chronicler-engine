# Memory — Story So Far

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate, Islaport, after grandfather Bernard's death. Bodyguard Carla pledges to guard him. Carla checks his phone login history: an entry three days ago, after Julian booked his train, from near Islaport. Julian had told only his mother. Carla says lock it, don't clear history; she wants the timestamp.

**Master key:** one passcode Bernard held, opening all estate locks. Handoff: vault room off the old dispensary; lawyer Campion witnesses, Jezebel runs it, new code from a sealed slip, slip destroyed after. Carla says Jezebel is an ally; keep the login from her one day to see if it leaks.

**Marble hall:** Gabriella (lavender gown, pearls) offers a tour, mentions a draft in the east wing. Julian declines; says he'll walk the house himself. She'll be in the west parlor this afternoon. Carla notes there is no draft — a warning or lure; they don't bite today.

**Jezebel's office (Julian, Carla, Jezebel):** Julian asks to see the inheritance and key procedure. Jezebel reveals a locksmith serviced the vault four years ago with the door propped open and the slip in hand; she signed off. Julian tells her the login. Jezebel names confidants: Bernard's brother Edmund (dead twelve years), Campion (paperwork only), and Marguerite, Bernard's nurse for his last two years. Bernard wrote everything down — a log in the bottom drawer of his private desk in the study. Jezebel never opened it.

**Study (Julian, Carla at door, Jezebel):** Jezebel unlocks the bottom drawer — empty. Julian checks for a false bottom; nothing. Jezebel says Bernard kept things in two places: this desk and the reading chair in the library. Under the chair's green cushion: a small brass key with a tag stamped *Hollis* in Bernard's hand.

**Jezebel:** Hollis is Marguerite Hollis, Edmund's widow, on Marchand Street, green garden gate. Bernard sent her money quarterly for twelve years, never missed, modest sums; last payment noted "For the boy's schooling." There is a son, near eighteen now. Payments went through Marchand Mutual bank. Jezebel will ring the bank before noon to see if the last payment cleared or sits uncollected.

**Plan:** Julian, with Carla, takes the carriage to Marchand Street today, before word spreads the drawer was opened. Line to tell: Bernard left Julian the key with her name on it — nothing about the drawer or the login. If she asks why now: her name turned up among Bernard's things. Jezebel's advice: don't mention the drawer; mean what he says — Marguerite was a nurse and reads faces. Carla's advice: knock, say who he is and why in one breath, leave a gap; if the son answers, cards down plain — the key and the money — before he asks.

**Known to whom:** Carla and Jezebel know the login, the procedure, the empty drawer, the key, and the Hollis payments. Gabriella knows nothing of these.

**Day 1, midday — Marchand Street (Julian, Carla, Tomas, Marguerite):** Julian tells the gate staff he goes to call on a widow of the family name; the maid and gatekeeper will tell it straight, after the carriage is out of sight. Carla notes the gatekeeper has a cousin on the east side. At the green gate Carla stays at the gate; Tomas Hollis (tall, thin, near eighteen, dirt to the wrist, pruning knife) answers. Julian gives his name, the key with Marguerite's name, and asks what it opens. Tomas lets him in; Julian wipes his boots, says the key stays with him even if she refuses the visit. Marguerite (gray hair, shawl, nurse's manner) receives him. Julian tells her of the twelve years of quarterly payments and the key; she says the key is to Edmund's strongbox, held by Islaport Trust since the old Marchand Mutual branch closed; she never had money or stomach for a court. She says the box belongs to Tomas. Julian says Tomas should turn the key; asks what paper names the box — neither knows. Julian proposes going to the Trust tomorrow with Tomas, paying for a lawyer if needed; Tomas agrees to meet at the Trust at **one o'clock tomorrow (Day 2)**, bringing the key; Julian brings the lawyer's name and the will. Tomas warns his mother will argue against hoping overnight; if he's talked round, he'll send word to the estate.

**Now:** carriage back at the estate gate. Gatekeeper hands Julian a folded note from Jezebel, to be given the moment he returned. Staff already know about Marchand, told straight. Carla says Jezebel will know a lawyer versed in the Trust's rulebook.
