**Memory — Day 1, late afternoon into evening (arrival day at Redmist Estate)**

- Earlier: Marchand Street visit done; Tomas Hollis keeps the brass key; meet at Islaport Trust 1 p.m. tomorrow. Carla's plan: Jezebel rings the bank, watch the account, lawyer who knows the Trust's rulebook.
- At the gate: Jezebel's note read by Julian, then Carla. Last quarterly draught cleared three weeks ago — after the funeral, before the will was read; sent on Bernard's standing, not Julian's; not collected since; account never formally closed. Jezebel advises keeping it open: closed leaves a trail, open leaves a door. Lawyer named: Halvorsen, old, methodical, has beaten the Trust's rulebook. Jezebel rings him tonight.
- Lisette met them at the doors: Campion rang, wants Julian before six (after six he takes the receiver off). Lisette led them to a study off the south corridor — Bernard's old sitting room, fire lit, decanter, telephone on the desk; Carla had it sorted that morning.
- Julian rang Campion (Carla present, listening). Campion: master-key handoff Friday, 10 a.m., before offices open; bring nothing; vault needs only his key and Jezebel's. Will altered in Bernard's last month — a codicil, six lines, names no one, grants nothing, speaks of a trust established some years before, off the estate's books. Codicil read aloud: "To my solicitor, my accountant, and my grandson. The trust established in the winter of the year of the Draught belongs to no name that appears on the estate's books. Its beneficiaries are known only to me and to my banker. No inquiry into it is to be treated as suspicious. It is only Bernard tidying." Campion knew nothing of it until this afternoon; has sat on it since three; will not hold it past tonight. The Draught = winter the harbor froze, 1905; city counts years from it. Bernard kept accounts at Marchand Mutual, then Islaport Trust; oldest account left open. Codicil stays quiet; Halvorsen hears it from Julian.
- Carla: Friday gives two days. Decision: Tomas hears the codicil before the Trust, from paper, not Julian's voice. Carla takes the note to Tomas tonight — he to be at the bench opposite the Trust at 12:30; if his mother talks him out, that is an answer too. Carla will not spend the evening persuading him; brings the answer either way. Route: garden side gate by the kitchen onto the back lane, hedge both sides, not the front drive. Julian's message to Tomas if asked: the truth — he doesn't outsource his apologies.
- Halvorsen will see Julian 8 a.m. tomorrow, King Street; one hour; leave the estate by 7:30. Carla's read on him: old money, older manners, offers tea he doesn't want and judges how the cup is held; measures, doesn't warm; tell the Marchand story whole; don't fill his silences; if he likes Julian he'll talk about next week.
- Supper with Louise (coming up with her notebook and kitchen list). Clothes for morning: dark, sober, nothing that shines.
- Lisette's warnings: east corridor clock runs nine minutes fast (Bernard set it); staff keep kitchen time — don't trust it for eight. A bell rings when harbor fog comes through the west study window, which doesn't shut true; on foggy nights it sounds like knocking — it is the wind.
- Carla gone to the kitchens, then to Tomas with the codicil.
