**Memory — Day 1, late morning (arrival day at Redmist Estate)**

- Earlier: Julian's phone showed a login three days ago; Carla says lock it, keep history, she wants the timestamp. Master key handoff: Jezebel runs it, lawyer Campion witnesses, new code set in vault room from a sealed slip. Carla: keep the login from Jezebel one day. Gabriella's "east wing draft" warning — no such draft; not investigated.
- Jezebel's office (Julian, Carla, Jezebel): four years ago a retired locksmith serviced the vault with the door propped open and the slip in hand; Jezebel signed off. Bernard's trusted few: Edmund (dead twelve years), Campion (paperwork only), Marguerite his nurse. Bernard's log was in the bottom drawer of his study desk.
- Study (Julian, Jezebel; Carla at the door): drawer unlocked and empty. Jezebel: if the log is gone or rearranged, go to Campion first, not police — no scandal.
- Library reading chair: under the green cushion, a small brass key tagged *Hollis*, stamped in Bernard's hand. Jezebel: Marguerite Hollis, Edmund's widow, lives on Marchand Street, green garden gate; Bernard sent her money quarterly twelve years, she never replied. Ledger: modest sums, last note "For the boy's schooling." There is a son, near eighteen now.
- Plan: Julian and Carla take the carriage to Marchand Street today, before word spreads. Line agreed: Bernard left Julian the key; nothing about the drawer or the login. Jezebel to ring Marchand Mutual (the paying bank, slip of paper in Julian's pocket with the key) before they return, to learn if the last quarterly draught cleared or sits uncollected.
- Carla's counsel: knock, say who he is and why in one breath, then leave a gap. Widow gets the gap; the son gets the facts of key and money plain and early. Carla hangs back a step at the door.
- Marchand Street, end of the row: chalky green gate, blue door, polished knocker. A tall thin young man, dirt to the wrist, pruning knife in hand, Redmist about the brow, stepped out: "Can I help you?"
