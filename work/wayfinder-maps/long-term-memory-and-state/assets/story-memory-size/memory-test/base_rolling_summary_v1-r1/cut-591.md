**Memory — Day 1, midday to early afternoon (arrival day at Redmist Estate)**

- Earlier: Julian's phone showed a login three days ago; Carla says lock it, keep history, she wants the timestamp. Master key handoff: Jezebel runs it, lawyer Campion witnesses, new code set in vault room from a sealed slip. Carla: keep the login from Jezebel one day. Gabriella's "east wing draft" warning — no such draft; not investigated.
- Jezebel's office (Julian, Carla, Jezebel): four years ago a retired locksmith serviced the vault with the door propped open and the slip in hand; Jezebel signed off. Bernard's trusted few: Edmund (dead twelve years), Campion (paperwork only), Marguerite his nurse. Bernard's log was in the bottom drawer of his study desk.
- Study (Julian, Jezebel; Carla at the door): drawer unlocked and empty. Jezebel: if the log is gone or rearranged, go to Campion first, not police — no scandal.
- Library reading chair: under the green cushion, a small brass key tagged *Hollis*, stamped in Bernard's hand. Jezebel: Marguerite Hollis, Edmund's widow, lives on Marchand Street, green garden gate; Bernard sent her money quarterly twelve years, she never replied. Ledger: modest sums, last note "For the boy's schooling." There is a son, near eighteen now.
- Plan: Julian and Carla take the carriage to Marchand Street today, before word spreads. Line agreed: Bernard left Julian the key; nothing about the drawer or the login. Jezebel to ring Marchand Mutual (the paying bank, slip of paper in Julian's pocket with the key) before they return, to learn if the last quarterly draught cleared or sits uncollected.
- Carla's counsel: knock, say who he is and why in one breath, then leave a gap. Widow gets the gap; the son gets the facts of key and money plain and early. Carla hangs back a step at the door.
- At the gate (Julian, maid, gatekeeper): Julian said he goes to Marchand Street, east side, and won't ask staff to lie — tell it straight, but wait until the carriage is out of sight. Maid: half the staff is from the east side; word will travel by tonight. Gatekeeper has a cousin on the east side (Carla noted his face). Carla: small city.
- In the carriage (Julian, Carla): bank left to Jezebel — she rings as the estate's accountant; two strangers asking at a bank writes its own story. Driver stops short of the house; Julian walks up. Carla stays at the gate.
- Marchand Street, end of the row: chalky green gate, blue door. Tomas Hollis (tall, thin, dirt to the wrist, trowel, Redmist brow) answered. Julian gave his name and the key in one breath, then the gap. Tomas: "Hollis was my father's family. She kept the name." Admitted him; "wipe your feet."
- Hall, then parlor (Julian; Tomas in and out; Marguerite Hollis by the window, shawl, gray hair pinned, nurse's manner): she thought the will a rumor. Julian told her: the will is real; twelve years of quarterly payments, last noted "for the boy's schooling," in Bernard's hand, in a book only Julian and one other have read; set the key, tag up, on the table. She said: it is Edmund's strongbox; the bank held it after his death and would not open it to her without a court; Bernard promised to sort it, then grew too sick to leave the house; the quarterly money came and he never wrote; the silence was her mistake. The box belongs to Tomas, not her.
- Julian: Tomas should turn the key. Asked which bank. Marguerite: Marchand Mutual held Edmund's accounts; the strongbox went to the Islaport Trust when the old branch closed years back — bigger, takes instructions, a deputy manager with a rulebook, court order unless the paper names Julian. Tomas asked whether Bernard put Julian's or Tomas's name on the box; Julian: doesn't know; the will named him the fortune but no strongbox and no Tomas; the lawyer who wrote it may not have known.
- Proposal (Julian): go to the Trust tomorrow with Tomas at his side; if paper is wanted, ask what paper; Julian pays for a lawyer, not Marguerite. Tomas may keep the key and go alone if he prefers.
- Agreed: meet at the Islaport Trust at one o'clock tomorrow afternoon (clerk's window opens nine, queue of shopkeepers; afternoons quiet). Tomas keeps the key; Julian brings the lawyer's name and whatever paper the will gave him. Tomas on the step: his mother will spend tonight arguing it's a mistake — she's against hoping, not against Julian. Julian: if she talks him round, send word to the estate.
- Back at the carriage (Julian, Carla): street quiet, cousin didn't beat them. Carla's read: the boy is steady, steadier than expected; Marguerite won't fuss over the key. Plan: Jezebel rings the bank; find whether the last draught was collected; put word among the Trust's staff that a Redmist asks after Hollis by name tomorrow. Julian's read: Tomas decided for himself and will hold to it, but has no experience with men like deputy managers — Julian will watch the deputy, not the box.
- Carla: they need a lawyer who knows the Trust's rulebook; Jezebel will know who.
- Back at the estate gate: gatekeeper handed Julian a folded note from Jezebel ("give it the moment you're back") and said the staff were told about Marchand straight, as Julian asked. Note not yet read.
