**Memory — Day 1, evening into Day 2, before dawn**

- Supper plan with Louise (kitchen corridor): Julian asked for hot, honest, light food before an 8 a.m. lawyer's office. Louise: fish stew is her "nobody watching" dish, but for the morning — fish, greens, bone broth, no cream. Kitchen keeps a pot 6 a.m.–10 p.m.; cold plates under cloth between; ring down before 7 if out past ten. Julian told her his movements: 8 a.m. Halvorsen, King Street; 1 p.m. Islaport Trust; if no word by seven, hot food on return. Louise wrote it in her book. She said it's not a favor, it's a kitchen.
- Julian went up to the master quarters: windows opened to sea air, fire laid unlit, dark wool suit and spare shirt laid out for morning. He shut the sash, changed in the lit dressing room, sat at the writing table with paper and ink; wrote nothing settled (one drip blotted).
- Carla knocked: Tomas had come to the house himself, would not take the codicil in the lane; in the library, key with him. Julian had Carla walk ahead, not beside.
- Library, fire lit. Present: Julian, Tomas, Carla (at the doors). Tomas: his mother's voice told him to sleep on it; "I slept on the strongbox for twelve years." He gave Julian a folded paper in his own hand — what his father told him before dying, corroborated by his mother.
- Tomas's account of the Draught (1905, harbor froze): Bernard came round one night with a young doctor from the hospital and a list; till dawn they went through who was short of coal, who had children, which boats were worth saving. Tomas's father kept the accounts in a book — "the only honest ledger he ever kept."
- Julian: the father's ledger is the paper wanted, not Tomas's note; Bernard kept journals too (on the library shelves). Asked if the book exists and if his mother has it. Tomas: it exists, in his mother's chest in her room with letters and death notices; she has never let him read it in twelve years ("not for me until I was a man grown").
- Julian told Tomas to ask her tonight — as the son kept from his father's hand, not for a banker's desk. Strongbox still goes to the Trust at 1 p.m. tomorrow, book or no book; if she refuses, ask the deputy manager to open it without the ledger. Tomas left by the side gate; will be short with Julian at one o'clock if he's holding his father's book at last.
- Julian asked Carla whether he has any right to want the ledger. Carla: right to ask, not to have; a widow's bedroom is her bedroom; want it, ask, take no until there's a court or the son's word; a Redmist who takes a book from a woman's chest is talked about by breakfast. Julian agreed; decided to go to Marchand Street in the morning before Halvorsen — not for the book, for the asking, to hear her reasons and her no to his face. Carla: half six, carriage round; take a no gently.
- Day 2, before dawn: Julian down before the hall lamps were up (proper clock 5:30), dark suit, coat over arm, hat; stood before the portrait of the Redmist who built the east wing. Carla came from the kitchen with a lantern: carriage at the gate early; noted the family's four generations of surgeons and no one able to keep a house in order. Fog, wet cobbles, low tide and cold iron; drove through the dark city, lamps on Marchand Street burning to stubs.
- At the green gate: one window lit. Julian stepped down, came up the path, stopped short of the gate in the lamplight, did not unlatch. Called up to the window: no knocking at this hour; came to ask to her face and hear the no to his face; nobody's arm needs twisting. (Carla present; driver in the carriage.)
