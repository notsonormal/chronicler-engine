**Memory — Day 1, morning (arrival day at Redmist Estate)**

- Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate; Carla introduces herself as his bodyguard, inside and outside the estate. She asks to borrow his phone for threat assessment.
- Julian keeps the phone in his hand: "I hold it, you look." He unlocks only what she needs.
- Login history shows an entry from three days ago — after Julian booked his train, before he told anyone but his mother he was coming. Carla: someone knows his timeline; may have shadowed the booking or pulled it from the rail company. She says lock the phone, don't clear history, she wants a copy of the timestamp.
- Carla explains the master key: one passcode Bernard set up, opens every estate lock — accounts, foundation books, vault, doors. Handoff procedure: Jezebel runs it, lawyer Campion witnesses, new code set in the vault room (off the old dispensary) from a sealed slip only Bernard read, destroyed after. Carla says Jezebel is an ally, not a suspect; keep the login from her a day to see if it leaks.
- Plan: ask Jezebel straight for the procedure end to end; Julian plays the nervous heir wanting to see the locks work; Carla signals if a step doesn't match what she remembers.
- In the marble hallway, Gabriella (lavender gown, pearls) intercepts, offers to tour the house, mentions a draft in the east wing. Julian declines — business with Jezebel first. Carla notes there is no draft in the east wing; it's a warning or lure. They don't investigate today.
- In Jezebel's east-wing office (present: Julian, Carla, Jezebel). Julian asks about accounts, foundations, and the master key handoff. Jezebel asks who told him; Julian says an old friend of Bernard's who knew how he thought. She reveals: four years ago a retired locksmith serviced the vault mechanism with the door propped open and the slip in hand; Jezebel signed off on the visit.
- Julian tells Jezebel about the login three days ago. She names those Bernard might have trusted: his brother Edmund (dead twelve years), solicitor Campion (paperwork only), and Marguerite, his nurse for his last two years, who was told things Jezebel wasn't.
- Jezebel says Bernard wrote everything down — a log in the bottom drawer of his private desk in the study. She never opened it; says Julian may have cause now.
- Facts known to: Carla and Julian — the login, the master key details, Gabriella's "draft" warning. Jezebel — the login and Julian's concerns; she does not know about Gabriella's warning.
