# Memory: Story So Far

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate, Islaport, as new heir after grandfather Bernard's death.

**Carla** (bodyguard, hired for Julian) meets him at the gate. She asks to borrow his phone for threat assessment; he insists he holds it and unlocks only what she needs. They check login history: an entry from **three days ago**, after Julian booked his train but before he told anyone but his mother he was coming. Carla says someone knows his timeline — either shadowed his booking or pulled it from the rail company. She tells him not to clear history; she wants a copy of the timestamp.

**Master key:** one passcode Bernard set up years ago opening every estate lock — accounts, foundation books, vault, doors. Only Bernard held it; it passes to Julian via a procedure: **Jezebel** runs it, lawyer **Campion** witnesses, new code set in the vault room (off the old dispensary) from a sealed slip only Bernard read, destroyed after. Carla says Jezebel is an ally, not a suspect; keep the login secret from her a day to see if it leaks from her office. Plan: ask Jezebel straight about the procedure, framing Julian as a nervous heir wanting to see the locks work. Carla will signal if a step doesn't match what she remembers.

**In the marble hallway**, **Gabriella** (lavender gown, pearls) intercepts Julian, offers to tour the house, mentions a draft in the east wing. Julian declines politely; says he has business with Jezebel first. Gabriella retreats to the **west parlor this afternoon**. Carla notes there is no draft in the east wing — it's a warning or lure; they won't investigate today.

**Jezebel's office, east wing** (present: Julian, Carla, Jezebel). Julian asks to see the inheritance paperwork and the master-key procedure end to end. Jezebel reveals: **four years ago a locksmith** (old, retired, from the city) serviced the mechanism with the door propped open and had the slip in hand; Jezebel signed off on the visit. Julian admits the three-days-ago login on his account. Jezebel names possible confidants: Bernard's brother **Edmund** (dead twelve years), solicitor **Campion** (paperwork only), and **Marguerite**, Bernard's nurse for his last two years, whom Bernard told things he wouldn't tell Jezebel. She says Bernard wrote everything down — a log in the **bottom drawer of his private desk in the study**. She never opened it; says Julian may have cause now.

**Open threads:** copy the login timestamp; find whether the handoff procedure is compromised; question Jezebel's office for leaks; the locksmith lead; Marguerite; Bernard's desk log; Gabriella's east-wing warning and west parlor meeting this afternoon; Julian to walk the house on his own time.
