# Memory: Story So Far

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate, Islaport, heir after grandfather Bernard's death. Carla (bodyguard) meets him at the gate.

**Login:** Julian's account shows an entry from **three days ago** — after he booked his train, before telling anyone but his mother. Carla wants a copy of the timestamp; keep it from Jezebel a day to see if it leaks from her office.

**Master key:** one passcode Bernard set years ago opens every estate lock. Handoff: **Jezebel** runs it, lawyer **Campion** witnesses, new code set in the vault room from a sealed slip only Bernard read, destroyed after. Carla says Jezebel is an ally.

**Marble hallway:** **Gabriella** (lavender gown, pearls) offers a tour, mentions a draft in the east wing. Julian declines. Gabriella retreats to the **west parlor this afternoon**. Carla: there is no draft — a warning or lure; not investigated today.

**Jezebel's office, east wing** (Julian, Carla, Jezebel). Four years ago a **locksmith** (old, retired, from the city) serviced the mechanism with the door propped open and the slip in hand; Jezebel signed off. Julian admits the login. Jezebel names confidants: Bernard's brother **Edmund** (dead twelve years), **Campion** (paperwork only), **Marguerite**, Bernard's nurse for his last two years. Bernard wrote everything in a log in the **bottom drawer of his private desk in the study**.

**The study** (Julian, Carla at door, Jezebel). Bottom drawer **empty**. Bernard's other hiding place: the reading chair in the library (a cushion he wouldn't let anyone mend).

**The library.** Under the green cushion: a **small brass key** tagged **Hollis**. Jezebel: **Marguerite Hollis**, Edmund's widow, lives on **Marchand Street**; Bernard sent her money every quarter, quiet, and she never wrote back. He wanted the key found by one of them.

**Ledger:** quarterly payments twelve years running, modest. Note by the last: **"For the boy's schooling."** Hollis has a son, now near **eighteen**. Payments went through **Marchand Mutual**, a small bank near her house.

**Plan:** Julian goes to Marchand Street today, before word spreads the drawer was opened. Line agreed: **Bernard left him the key with her name on it** — nothing about the drawer or the login. Jezebel will **ring Marchand Mutual before noon** to see if the last payment cleared or sits uncollected, and report before they return; Julian also wants to know who holds the paying account and whether payments stopped since Bernard's death.

**Warnings:** Marguerite was a nurse and will read his face — mean what he says. The son, if grown, is old enough to have opinions. Carla: take the carriage; at the door, say who he is and why in one breath, then leave a gap; if the son answers, cards down plain and early.

**Gates, before leaving** (Julian, maid, gatekeeper). Julian says he has business on Marchand Street, east side, and asks staff to say it plain if they'll send word ahead. Maid: half the staff is local, east side especially; word will travel by tonight. Gatekeeper: the lady (Gabriella) has been here two weeks and says plenty the staff hear. Julian: tell it straight — the master went to call on a widow of the family name; wait until the carriage is out of sight. Carla notes the gatekeeper has a cousin on the east side.

**Marchand Street, late morning** (Julian, Carla). Green gate, sagging hinge, blue door, polished knocker. Carla waits at the gate. **Tomas Hollis** answers — tall, thin, trowel in hand, the Redmist brow. Julian gives his name, the key with his mother's name on it, and asks what it opens. Tomas lets him in; says "Hollis was my father's family. She kept the name." Julian wipes his boots, says if she doesn't want him past the hall he'll go and the key stays with him either way. Tomas: "Key stays with you regardless. She'll like that."

**Parlor** (Julian, Marguerite, Tomas at the door). Marguerite: "Bernard's grandson." Julian tells her of the twelve years of payments, the schooling note, and sets the key on the table. Marguerite: the key is to **Edmund's strongbox**; the bank held it after Edmund died and wouldn't open it to her without a court. Bernard said he'd sort it, then got too sick; the quarterly money came and he never wrote. "It's the boy's now." Julian: Tomas should turn the key. Asks which bank. Marguerite: **Marchand Mutual** held Edmund's accounts; the strongbox went to the **Islaport Trust** when the old branch closed. Bigger; a deputy manager with a rulebook; a court order unless the paper names Julian. Tomas asks whether Bernard put Julian's or Tomas's name on the box. Julian: doesn't know; the will named him the fortune but no strongbox and no Tomas; the lawyer may not have known. Proposal: go to the Trust tomorrow with Tomas at his side; if paper is wanted, ask what paper, and Julian pays for a lawyer. Or Tomas keeps the key and goes alone.

**Agreed:** Tomas keeps the key. Meet at the **Islaport Trust at one o'clock tomorrow afternoon** (mornings are crowded). Julian brings the lawyer's name and whatever paper the will gave him. Tomas on the step: his mother will spend tonight convincing herself it's a mistake — she's against hoping, not against Julian.

**Carriage back** (Julian, Carla). Carla: the boy is steady; Marguerite won't make a fuss. Time until one o'clock: Jezebel's bank call, and word put out among the Trust's staff that a Redmist asks after Hollis tomorrow. Julian: Tomas will hold to a decision, but has no experience with men like deputy managers; Julian will watch the deputy, not the box. Carla: they need a lawyer who knows the Trust's rulebook — Jezebel will know who.

**Back at the estate, midday.** Gatekeeper hands Julian a **folded note from Jezebel**, to be given the moment he returned. Staff know about Marchand, told straight as asked.

**Jezebel's note** (read by Julian, then Carla, in the hall). The last payment cleared **three weeks ago** — after the funeral, before the will was read; sent on Bernard's standing, not Julian's. Not collected since, but the account was never formally closed; she counsels **not to close it yet** — a closed account leaves a trail, an open one leaves a door. Lawyer named: **Halvorsen** — old, methodical, has beaten the Trust's rulebook. He will want the will in hand and a client whose name he can say without flinching; Jezebel will ring him tonight. Carla: watch the open account a week to learn who signs the draughts now.

**Lisette at the doors:** **Campion** rang, wants a call back before the evening (after six he takes the receiver off).

**Bernard's sitting room, south corridor** (Julian, Carla; fire lit, decanter, telephone on desk; sorted by Carla that morning). Julian rings Campion, Carla listening. Campion, brief: (1) the **master-key handoff cannot wait past the week** — every day it sits is a day someone can argue it wasn't properly transferred; (2) Bernard altered his will in his last month — a **codicil, six lines**, naming no one, granting nothing, speaking of a **trust established some years before, off the estate's books**. Julian asks for the exact words and whether Campion knew. Codicil: *"To my solicitor, my accountant, and my grandson. The trust established in the winter of the year of the Draught belongs to no name that appears on the estate's books. Its beneficiaries are known only to me and to my banker. No inquiry into it is to be treated as suspicious. It is only Bernard tidying."* Campion knew nothing until he read it aloud that afternoon (three o'clock); would not have held it past tonight.

**The Draught:** the winter the Islaport harbor froze — **1905**; the city still counts years from it. Julian: too early for Tomas — a different boy, a different trust; the codicil is about whatever door Bernard opened that winter and never closed. Campion: "my banker," singular; Bernard kept accounts at Marchand Mutual until it closed, then Islaport Trust — a man who leaves the oldest account open means the old door to still work.

**Handoff set: Friday, ten in the morning**, before offices open. Julian brings only himself; the slip is in the vault, opened with Campion's key and Jezebel's. Codicil stays quiet; Halvorsen hears it from Julian, not Campion's clerk.
