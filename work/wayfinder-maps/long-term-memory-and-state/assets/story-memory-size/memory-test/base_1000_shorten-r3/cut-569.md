# Memory: Story So Far

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate, Islaport, as new heir after grandfather Bernard's death.

**Carla** (bodyguard) meets him at the gate. Login history on Julian's account shows an entry from **three days ago** — after he booked his train, before he told anyone but his mother. Carla says someone knows his timeline; keep the history, she wants a copy of the timestamp.

**Master key:** one passcode Bernard set up years ago opening every estate lock. Passes to Julian via a procedure: **Jezebel** runs it, lawyer **Campion** witnesses, new code set in the vault room from a sealed slip only Bernard read, destroyed after. Carla says Jezebel is an ally; keep the login secret from her a day to see if it leaks from her office.

**Marble hallway:** **Gabriella** (lavender gown, pearls) offers Julian a tour, mentions a draft in the east wing. He declines, says he has business with Jezebel first. Gabriella retreats to the **west parlor this afternoon**. Carla notes there is no draft in the east wing — a warning or lure; they won't investigate today.

**Jezebel's office, east wing** (Julian, Carla, Jezebel). Jezebel reveals: **four years ago a locksmith** (old, retired, from the city) serviced the mechanism with the door propped open and the slip in hand; Jezebel signed off on the visit. Julian admits the three-days-ago login. Jezebel names possible confidants: Bernard's brother **Edmund** (dead twelve years), **Campion** (paperwork only), and **Marguerite**, Bernard's nurse for his last two years. She says Bernard wrote everything down — a log in the **bottom drawer of his private desk in the study**.

**The study** (Julian, Carla at the door, Jezebel). Jezebel unlocks the bottom drawer: **empty**, not even a scrap of paper. Julian checks for a false bottom; nothing. He asks where else Bernard kept things. Jezebel: two places — this desk, and the reading chair in the library (a cushion Bernard wouldn't let anyone mend).

**The library.** Jezebel lifts the green cushion: beneath it a **small brass key** with a tag stamped **Hollis**. Jezebel: not a lock in this house — **Marguerite Hollis**, Edmund's widow, lives on **Marchand Street** in town. Bernard sent her money every quarter, quiet, and she never wrote back. He wanted the key found by one of them, not a stranger.

**Ledger:** quarterly payments twelve years running, modest (enough for a small household, not comfort). Note beside the last payment: **"For the boy's schooling."** Hollis had a son — now near **eighteen**. Payments went through **Marchand Mutual**, a small bank near her house.

**Plan:** Julian goes to Marchand Street today, before word spreads that the drawer was opened. Line agreed with Carla: **Bernard left him the key with her name on it** — nothing about the drawer, nothing about the login. If she asks why now, he tells the truth as far as it goes. Jezebel advises not mentioning the drawer; let her wonder what else Bernard left. Jezebel will **ring Marchand Mutual before noon** to see if the last payment cleared or sits uncollected, and report before they return. Julian also wants to know who holds the paying account and whether payments have stopped since Bernard's death — the boy's tuition may be the first thing someone reaches for.

**Jezebel's warnings:** Marguerite was a nurse and will read his face faster than his words — mean whatever he says. The son, if grown, is old enough to have opinions about a fortune that didn't come to him.

**Carla's advice:** take the carriage, not a walk. At a widow's door: knock, say who he is and why he's come in one breath, then leave a gap. If the son answers: cards down plain and early — the fact of the key and the fact of the money; let him decide what to feel. If no one opens the door, that itself tells them something.

**Marchand Street, late morning** (Julian, Carla). Rows of modest brick houses, green and blue doors, gardens behind low walls. The Hollis house at the end of the row: green gate, chalky paint, sagging hinge; neat working garden, pots along the step; blue door, recently polished brass knocker. Julian goes to the door with Carla hanging back a step at the gate. Before he knocks, the door opens: a **tall, thin young man** steps out with a **pruning knife** in his hand, dirt to the wrist — his mother's plain face, something of the Redmist about the brow. He looks at Julian, then at Carla at the gate, shifts his grip on the knife, and asks: **"Can I help you?"**
