# Story Memory

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate, Islaport, after grandfather Bernard's death. Bodyguard Carla meets him at the gatehouse; she asks to inspect his phone, he holds it himself. Login history shows an entry from three days ago — after he booked his train, before he told anyone but his mother. Carla: someone knows his timeline; possibly shadowed his booking or pulled it from the rail company. She orders him to keep the history, not clear it.

**Master key:** one passcode Bernard set up years ago, opens every estate lock — accounts, foundation books, vault, doors. Was Bernard's failsafe; now passes to Julian. Procedure: vault room off the old dispensary; lawyer Campion witnesses, Jezebel witnesses; new code set on the spot from a sealed slip only Bernard ever read, slip destroyed after. Takes about an hour. Carla says Jezebel is honest, an ally not a suspect, but the room, witnesses and lawyer are all suspect until checked. Plan: ask Jezebel straight about the procedure, keep the login secret one day; if it leaks from her office, they'll know who's listening. Cover story: Julian is the nervous heir wanting to see the locks work. Handoff not yet scheduled.

**East wing hallway:** Gabriella intercepts, dressed in lavender silk and pearls, offers to tour him. Julian declines — business with Jezebel first, then he'll walk the house on his own time. Gabriella warns of a "draft" in the east wing. Carla notes there is no draft — it's a warning or a lure away from something. Plan: don't bite today; after Jezebel, find out what Gabriella doesn't want seen. Gabriella says she'll be in the west parlor this afternoon.

**Jezebel's office (east wing), same morning.** Present: Julian, Carla, Jezebel. Julian asks to see the inheritance and the master-key procedure end to end. Jezebel reveals: four years ago a retired locksmith from the city serviced the mechanism with the door propped open and the slip in hand; Jezebel signed off on the visit. Julian tells her about the login three days ago. Jezebel names possible confidants: Bernard's brother Edmund (dead twelve years), solicitor Campion (paperwork only), and Marguerite, Bernard's nurse for his last two years, whom he told things he wouldn't tell Jezebel. She says Bernard wrote everything down — a log in the bottom drawer of his private desk in the study. She has never opened it.

**Study, same morning.** Present: Julian, Carla (at the door), Jezebel. Jezebel unlocks the bottom drawer: empty, no scrap of paper. Julian checks for a false bottom; nothing. He asks where else Bernard kept things. Jezebel: two places — the desk and the reading chair in the library. In the library, under the green cushion of the wing-backed reading chair, they find a small brass key with a tag stamped *Hollis* in Bernard's hand. Jezebel: that's Edmund's widow, Marguerite Hollis, lives on Marchand Street in town, widow's house with a green garden gate. Bernard barely spoke of her; sent her money every quarter for twelve years, quiet, and she never wrote back. Julian decides to go to Marchand Street today, before word spreads that the drawer was opened; wants Carla and/or Jezebel with him.

**Ledger:** Jezebel shows the quarterly payments — modest, a few thousand each, never missed, no stated purpose. Note beside the last payment: "For the boy's schooling." There is a son, twelve years back — Edmund's son, near eighteen now, Julian's second cousin. Julian: tell her her son's name is in Bernard's hand; if the son answers, cards down plain (key and money), a widow gets the gap. He asks Jezebel to find who holds the paying account and whether payments have stopped. Bank: Marchand Mutual, small bank near her house. Jezebel will ring them before noon, quietly, framed as tidying estate affairs; Julian wants to know before they return whether the last payment cleared or sits uncollected.

**Line agreed:** Bernard left Julian the key with her name on it. Nothing about the drawer, nothing about the three-days-ago login. If she asks why now, tell the truth as far as it goes — her name turned up among Bernard's things.

**Departure:** Carla calls for the carriage; five minutes, stay where there's a wall at your back. Jezebel's advice: Marguerite was a nurse, will read his face faster than his words — mean it. Carla's coaching: knock, who you are, why you've come, in one breath; don't fill the silence; if she doesn't open the door, that tells them something. Julian carries the brass key and the bank slip in his pocket.

**Gate, late morning.** Julian tells the maid and gatekeeper plainly he's going to Marchand Street, east side, to call on a widow of the family name; asks them not to spread it until the carriage is out of sight. Maid: half the staff is from around here, east side especially; word will travel by tonight. Gatekeeper: "ma'am" (Gabriella) has been here two weeks and says plenty the staff hear. Carla, at the carriage: gatekeeper has a cousin on the east side; it's a small city.

**Marchand Street, late morning.** Julian and Carla arrive by carriage, stopping short of the house; Carla stays at the gate. Tomas Hollis, near eighteen, tall and thin, dirt to the wrist, trowel in hand, answers the blue door. Julian gives his name and says Bernard left him a key with Marguerite's name on it; asks if she's home. Tomas lets him in, tells him to wipe his feet, says wait in the hall, don't wander. Tomas tells his mother; she agrees to see him.

**Parlor, with Marguerite Hollis.** Present: Julian, Marguerite; Tomas in the doorway. Julian tells her of the twelve years of quarterly payments, the note "for the boy's schooling," and the key. Marguerite: the key is to Edmund's strongbox; the bank held it after Edmund died and wouldn't open it to her without a court; Bernard said he'd see it sorted before he got too sick to leave the house. She says whatever's inside belongs to Tomas, not her. She says Marchand Mutual held Edmund's accounts, but the strongbox went to the Islaport Trust when the old branch closed — bigger, rulebook, needs a court order unless the paper names Julian. Julian admits he doesn't know whether the box is in his name or Tomas's; the will named him the fortune but no strongbox and no Tomas. Julian proposes: go to the Trust tomorrow with Tomas at his side; if paper is needed, ask what paper, and Julian pays for a lawyer — his coin, not Marguerite's. Tomas may keep the key and go alone if he prefers.

**Agreed:** Tomas keeps the key. Meet at the Islaport Trust at **one o'clock tomorrow (Day 2) afternoon** — mornings are too busy at the clerk's window. Tomas brings the key; Julian brings the lawyer's name and whatever paper the will gave him. Tomas, on the step: his mother will spend tonight arguing him out of it — she's against hoping, not against Julian.

**Carriage back to the estate.** Carla: the boy is steady; Marguerite won't make a fuss about the key. Time enough for Jezebel to ring the bank and to put word out at the Trust that a Redmist will ask after Hollis by name tomorrow. Julian: Tomas decided on his own and will hold to it, but has no experience with men like deputy managers; Julian will watch the deputy, not the box. Carla: they need a lawyer who knows the Trust's rulebook — Jezebel will know who.

**Back at the estate, late morning.** The gatekeeper meets the carriage with a folded note from Jezebel, to be given the moment Julian returned. He reports the staff were told about Marchand straight, as Julian asked.

**Open threads:** contents of Jezebel's note (unread); the three-days-ago login; the locksmith and the exposed slip; Bernard's log missing from the study drawer; Gabriella's east-wing warning and the west parlor this afternoon; the master-key handoff not yet scheduled; Jezebel's call to Marchand Mutual before noon and whether the last payment cleared; the lawyer's name for the Trust; Carla to copy the login timestamp.
