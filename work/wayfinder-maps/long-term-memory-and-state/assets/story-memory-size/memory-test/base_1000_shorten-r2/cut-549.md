# Story Memory

**Day 1, morning.** Julian Redmist arrives at Redmist Estate, Islaport, after grandfather Bernard's death. Meets bodyguard Carla at the gatehouse. She asks to inspect his phone for threat assessment; he holds it himself, unlocks only what she needs. Login history shows an entry from three days ago — after he booked his train, before he told anyone but his mother he was coming. Carla: someone knows his timeline; possibly shadowed his booking or pulled it from the rail company. She orders him to keep the history, not clear it.

**Master key:** one passcode Bernard set up years ago, opens every estate lock — accounts, foundation books, vault, doors. Was Bernard's failsafe; now passes to Julian. Procedure: vault room off the old dispensary; lawyer Campion witnesses, Jezebel witnesses; new code set on the spot from a sealed slip only Bernard ever read, slip destroyed after. Takes about an hour. Carla says Jezebel is honest, an ally not a suspect, but the room, witnesses and lawyer are all suspect until checked. Plan: ask Jezebel straight about the procedure, keep the login secret one day; if it leaks from her office, they'll know who's listening. Cover story: Julian is the nervous heir wanting to see the locks work.

**East wing hallway:** Gabriella intercepts, dressed in lavender silk and pearls, offers to tour him. Julian declines — business with Jezebel first, then he'll walk the house on his own time. Gabriella warns of a "draft" in the east wing. Carla notes there is no draft — it's a warning or a lure away from something. Plan: don't bite today; after Jezebel, find out what Gabriella doesn't want seen. Gabriella says she'll be in the west parlor this afternoon.

**Jezebel's office (east wing), same morning.** Present: Julian, Carla, Jezebel. Julian asks to see the inheritance and the master-key procedure end to end. Jezebel reveals: four years ago a retired locksmith from the city serviced the mechanism with the door propped open and the slip in hand; Jezebel signed off on the visit. Julian tells her about the login three days ago. Jezebel names possible confidants: Bernard's brother Edmund (dead twelve years), solicitor Campion (paperwork only), and Marguerite, Bernard's nurse for his last two years, whom he told things he wouldn't tell Jezebel. She says Bernard wrote everything down — a log in the bottom drawer of his private desk in the study. She has never opened it; says Julian may have cause now.

**Open threads:** the three-days-ago login; the locksmith and the exposed slip; Marguerite; Bernard's log in the study desk; Gabriella's east-wing warning and the west parlor this afternoon; the master-key handoff not yet scheduled; Carla to copy the login timestamp.
