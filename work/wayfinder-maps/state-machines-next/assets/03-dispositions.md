## Answer

Draft for the parent session.

Of 15 machines of the server Game state, 9 get "leave it". B10 (Role health) and B11 (change counters) get "explicit in the code", each by a small change in one module. B2 (dock record) and B4 (encounters, with the B3 fire marks) get a model-based test, because several places make their moves. B1 (the two-step status read) and B12 (the config selection checked outside the hold) get a TLA+ extension of `verify-gate`, because each is a client-visible race that shares the busy registry or the config write. Four of these also get a generated diagram. Two bugs are filed: 10 (the Trigger choice follows the hash order) and 11 (a refused accept on a removed Game makes its counters again). The owner has 8 kept and 2 new behaviour questions. W2, W3 and W4 wait for their answers.
