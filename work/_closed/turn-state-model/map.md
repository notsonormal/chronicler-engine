# Map: turn state model

Labels: wayfinder:map

## Destination

A written model of a turn's states, the writers of game state, and the part of a turn each retry form re-runs, checked against the code. It ends with one decision: where a new design of the game-state lifecycle makes sense, area by area, and where today's design stays. The owner judges this on a sketch of the new design next to today's code. The way is clear when the [platform choice](../platform-choice/map.md) map can take that verdict with nothing about the lifecycle's design left to decide. That map decides how and where the result is built.

Until 2026-10-12 the decision read "targeted fixes or a rewrite of the turn pipeline". [Decide where the new design of the game-state lifecycle makes sense](issues/24-decide-fix-or-rewrite.md) changed it (session 1, Q1).
