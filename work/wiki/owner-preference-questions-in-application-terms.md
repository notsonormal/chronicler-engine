# Owner preference: ask in the terms of the application

- On 2026-11-11, in the grilling of [Choose the memory candidates to try](../wayfinder-maps/long-term-memory-and-state/issues/05-memory-candidates.md), the owner did not understand two rounds of questions. The first round asked what the memory test must measure. The owner's answer: the cause is known (the history is cut), so a question about the test reads as a search for a problem that is already known. The second round offered three designs with research names ("rolling summary", "scene notes", "search"). The owner's answer: they do not relate to the parts that the owner had already named (lorebooks, Character memory), and they do not look like a design that this application would use.
- The rule from this: a question to the owner names the parts of the application as the owner named them (story memory, Character memory, lore entry, card change, story clock). It says what the player or the author gets. A test method or a research name is not a decision for the owner. The coordinator decides those, and reports them.
- The rule from this: say which options the owner already chose, and which come from a coordinator draft. The three designs came from a draft of a paused session that the owner never answered.
- On 2026-11-11, a third round asked the owner to choose between a list of entries and one summary, between always-on and keyed entries, and what a Character memory does while the Character is absent. The owner's answer: the first was talked about before, and the others were talked about with no conclusion. The charting session of 2026-11-02 (`2026-11-02T23-41-16-764Z_01a19ea3-…jsonl`) confirms it. There the owner said: "I'm actually not entirely sure how the memory should work", and set the order research, then trials, then the design for this game. The list-or-summary question was left open on purpose, for the trials. The owner had answered the absent-Character question (Q13 of that session), and the map Notes hold the answer.
- The rule from this: when the owner set "research and trials decide", the coordinator chooses the design details from the research, states them, and lets the trial judge. The owner gets a go or no-go on a concrete implementation, and then results. The coordinator does not ask the owner to choose a design detail by opinion.
- The rule from this: before a question to the owner, check whether an earlier session asked it. The user messages of the charting session are the source. A broad `session_search` returned about 11,000 tokens of unrelated summaries. A read of the user messages of the three sessions that the map history names was cheaper. Give this search to a scout.
